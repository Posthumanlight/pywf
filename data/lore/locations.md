Evermarsh is the location within Shyrvana country, Middle Lands region it is a wetlands region, located where river Levina falls into river Alit. Many of elvish ruins are located there, including the city of Ir-Naem. Population - around 3-4 thousands of elves (primarily in Ir-Naem) and couple hundreds of humans in the villages around Evermarsh.
